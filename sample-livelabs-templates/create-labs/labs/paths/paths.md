# Paths

Choose the guide that fits how you want to build your LiveLabs workshop. Start with the **New Author Guide** for a guided journey and interactive tools, or follow the **Step-by-Step Guide** for detailed labs on the established GitHub workflow. Both cover workshop requests, content development, quality checks, and publication.

## Choose Your Guide

### New Author Guide

**A guided start, practical tools, and reference material in one place.**

The New Author Guide organizes the journey into three clear stages: **submit your workshop request, create your workshop, and review and publish**.

Use it to understand what to do next, prepare your request, and choose between NoDoc and GitHub for authoring.

<a href="https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/" style="display:inline-block;padding:0.65rem 1rem;background-color:#C74634;border:1px solid #C74634;border-radius:4px;color:#ffffff;font-weight:700;text-decoration:none;">Open Author Guide</a>

**What you can do:**

- **Follow a three-step Quickstart.** Move from your initial idea to a publishing request with guidance, checkpoints, and common mistakes to avoid at each stage.
- **Draft workshop request details faster.** Describe your workshop idea and generate example fields, including descriptions, to help complete your Workshop Management System (WMS) request. Review and adapt the generated text to match what participants will actually learn and build.
- **Explore the request and review process.** Use the interactive WMS status map to see what each status means, who is responsible, and what happens next, including review and rework.
- **Find answers as you work.** Search the Cheatsheet for focused explanations, checklists, and topic links without working through the full guide.
- **Look up Markdown patterns.** Find lab structure guidance, formatting examples, and copyable snippets for common LiveLabs content.
- **Build with NoDoc.** Create, organize, and review content in a browser-based workspace without managing GitHub forks, local clones, or pull requests yourself. The NoDoc guide covers browser editing, optional AI-assisted authoring through MCP, draft review, history, validation, and LiveLabs previews. Quality review and the WMS publishing request remain part of the process.

**Go directly to a topic:**

- **Start your workshop:** [Follow the three-step Quickstart](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/quickstart/).
- **Prepare your request:** [Generate workshop descriptions and request fields](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/quickstart/#prompt-examples) and [explore the interactive WMS workflow](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/quickstart/#status-flow).
- **Choose how to build:** [Compare NoDoc and GitHub authoring](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/quickstart/#choose-authoring-path) or [open the NoDoc guide](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/nodoc/).
- **Find a quick answer:** [Browse the Cheatsheet](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/cheatsheet/) and [look up Markdown examples](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/markdown/).
- **Review and publish:** [Prepare for quality review](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/quickstart/#quality-assurance-order) and [follow the publishing request walkthrough](https://oracle-livelabs.github.io/common/new-author-guide/quickstart/#publishing-request-walkthrough).

### Step-by-Step Guide

**Detailed, hands-on labs for the GitHub authoring workflow.**

Continue with this guide if you want to work directly with workshop files, Markdown, and GitHub. The labs walk through submitting a request, setting up your tools, keeping your repository in sync, developing content, checking quality, and publishing through WMS. You can follow them in order or open a specific topic when you need it.
